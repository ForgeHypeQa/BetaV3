Veggify: from a Shopify storeto an AI grocery app

A 12-item order: from about 30 minutes to about 5.

Veggify is an online grocery store in Doha that ran on Shopify. Customers struggled to fill their carts, and the team struggled to see their real costs. ForgeHype rebuilt it as one platform: AI-powered search for shoppers, and one admin for the team.

Visit veggify.net ↗Build one like this
[ The challenge ]

Slow to order.Hard to run.

  • A grocery list meant searching item by item. A 12-item order took about 30 minutes on average.
  • The store showed nothing about costs or operating expenses. Working out net profit meant juggling several separate apps and software.
  • Supplier purchase orders were checked and sent by hand.
  • Minimum order quantities and quantity discounts were hard to manage.
  • Duplicate products that had never been ordered once cluttered the catalogue.
[ What we built ]

One platform for shoppers.One admin for the team.

  • 01

    AI-powered search

    Customers type, speak or snap a photo of their list, and the basket fills itself. A 12-item order went from about 30 minutes to about 5.

  • 02

    Personal favourites

    Every customer gets their own favourites list, built from what they buy, so a regular order is a few taps.

  • 03

    Occasions and festive lists

    A party at home or a festive season: the app remembers what was needed last time and what other customers bought for the same occasion, so next year it is one tap.

  • 04

    Minimum orders and quantity discounts

    Built into product selection, so the right minimum and the right discount apply on their own.

  • 05

    Leads and abandoned carts

    The team sees which customers are looking at which items. Abandoned carts get an automatic follow-up.

  • 06

    Costs and net profit in one admin

    Staff enter purchases, expenses and collections in one place. Net profit is worked out for them, with no separate apps.

  • 07

    Purchase orders on WhatsApp

    Purchase orders go to each supplier’s WhatsApp automatically. Manual checking became a review of a few minutes.

  • 08

    A clean product list

    Duplicates and products never ordered were filtered out, leaving a catalogue that is easier to shop and to manage.

[ The result ]

Less time on admin.More time on growth.

With ordering, supplier orders and accounts automated, the Veggify team now spends its time on promotion instead of admin. Watch the old store and the new one side by side in the video at the top of this page.
