# Audience and Pain Points v1.0 (starter)

## Core pain points for video messaging (from the owner)
1. **No response.** The driver arrives and nobody answers. Wasted trip, missed delivery, frustrated customer.
2. **Heavy manual lifting in extreme weather.** Heat or rain, carrying heavy bottles, hauling empties back to the truck.
3. **Repetitive manual work that wastes energy, time and productivity.** Paper coupons, balance checks, answering calls, tracking deliveries.

## How the product answers them (from the Complete Panels PDF)
| Pain point | Product answer |
|---|---|
| No response | No-response flow: reason, optional door photo, customer notified by app, WhatsApp and email, coupons not deducted, one-tap reschedule |
| Heavy manual work | Route list in order, delivery window per stop, bottle counts, delivery note (for example leave at the side gate) |
| Repetitive work | Photo proof, automatic notifications, automatic dashboard updates, payment links, reports |
