## How the three panels connect (from the PDF)
1. A driver confirms a delivery with photo proof. The customer gets an app notification and a WhatsApp message with a reference.
2. The customer dashboard updates automatically: coupons drop, empties collected are recorded, history logs the delivery.
3. If nobody answers, the driver reports No response. Coupons are not deducted, the stop goes to the admin missed list and the customer can reschedule.
4. Admin monitors routes live, reschedules missed stops, follows up low-coupon customers and sends payment links.
