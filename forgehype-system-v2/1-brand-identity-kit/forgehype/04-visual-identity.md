TBC = owner to confirm. Sources: logo files, lockup sheet and Complete Panels PDF supplied by the owner.

## 1. Logo (official lockups)
Files in `/logos`. Use the files only. Never retype, redraw, recolor or stretch.

| Lockup | Look | Use on |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Orange "Forge" + white light-weight "Hype" | Dark backgrounds (dark scenes) |
| Reversed | All white | Orange backgrounds |
| Mono | All black | Light backgrounds (light scenes) |
| Stacked (Forge over Hype) | Same colors as Primary | Square or tight spaces. TBC: confirm it is official |

- Clear space: the height of the "F" on all sides. Minimum width 300px in 9:16.
- Placement: opening reveal ("Meet ForgeHype") and end card.
- Not official, so excluded: a heavier text-built wordmark, an orange-gradient "Forge" with black "Hype" on cream, and any "Qatar" add-on line. TBC if a "Qatar" lockup should exist.
- Still needed from owner: transparent SVG or PNG for Primary, Reversed and Mono. The supplied files are on black backgrounds, and the Mono lockup exists only inside the lockup sheet.

## 2. Color
- Accent orange: sampled `#FE5D02` from the lockup sheet and `#F85E16` from the logo PNG. Working value in theme.ts is `#FE5D02`. TBC exact value.
- Dark mode: near-black (`#0A0A0A`-`#121212`) with a soft warm glow at the top.
- Light mode: cream/peach (`#FFF7F0`).
- Product screenshots and the Complete Panels pages are shown unaltered. Their extra colors (green, purple, red, blue chips) belong to the product UI and are exempt from the palette.
- In graphics outside product screens: orange plus neutrals only.

## 3. Typography
- English headline: heavy condensed sans, UPPERCASE (as in "AI COSTS" and "ONE TEAM. TWO SHIFTS."). Nearest free match: Anton. TBC exact font.
- Small bracket labels in a mono face, for example `[ OFFICE TIMING ]`, appear across ForgeHype material. Allowed, one per scene.
- English body and captions: clean grotesque sans.
- Bengali: Hind Siliguri bold, letterSpacing 0, split only at spaces.
- One keyword per headline in orange.

## 4. UI mockups
- Customer app is dark, driver and admin panels are light. In scenes, show each panel in its own look.
- Dark background: dark UI surfaces with a light hairline and soft shadow so the UI separates from the background.
- Light background: white surface, visible shadow, darker device outline.
- Instagram and Meta (Facebook) Reels are the primary targets. Check separation on a phone-size still.
- Names and numbers inside product screens are demo data. Do not quote them in the voiceover as real results.
