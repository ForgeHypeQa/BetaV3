# Motion Rulebook (calm, fluid, one language)

## Timing (30fps)
| Item | Frames | Seconds |
|---|---|---|
| Scene length | 150-180 | 5-6 |
| Entrance | 18 | 0.6 |
| Keyword or state swap | 12 | 0.4 |
| Exit | 12 | 0.4 |
| Mode change dissolve | 15 | 0.5 |
| Minimum hold of any text after it appears | 60 | 2 |

## The only motion allowed
1. **Entrance:** fade in with a 24px rise. Same direction for every element.
2. **Stagger:** at most 6 frames between elements, in reading order.
3. **Swap:** crossfade the keyword, icon, image or UI state in place. The rest stays still.
4. **Exit:** fade out.
5. **Bar fill / count up:** only on stat scenes, slow and steady.
One easing curve for everything.

## Banned
Sliding in from the sides or random directions, scale pops and overshoot, 3D tilt, floating loops, rotation, shake, glitch, particles, wipes and fast cuts.

## Timing to voiceover
- The visual for a line appears up to 6 frames before the voiceover cue, never earlier.
- The keyword swap lands on the voiceover word.
- Leave a clear moment at the end of each scene with nothing moving.
