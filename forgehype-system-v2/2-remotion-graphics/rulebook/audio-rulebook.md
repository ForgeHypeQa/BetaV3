# Audio Rulebook

The voiceover leads. Sound design stays out of its way.

- Voiceover at full level. Everything else sits well below it.
- Sound effects only at scene changes: at most one soft sound per scene change, volume 0.12-0.2 of the voiceover.
- None on text, cards, icons, checkmarks or buttons.
- No pops, clicks, whooshes or stacked sounds. Maximum 3 sound files in a project.
- Optional end chime on the logo end card, soft and single.
- Background music is off by default. If used: volume 0.08 or lower, ducked under the voiceover.
- Gate 3: Claude lists the final sound plan (what, where, volume) and asks for confirmation before motion.
